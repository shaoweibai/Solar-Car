All calculations update with the inputs. Trigonometric angles are converted to radians internally. Front-view arm lengths are projected effective lengths, not individual 3D A-arm tube lengths.
Model limits: The optimization is a single-degree-of-freedom choice of upper inner-pivot height, with both inner pivots on the same vertical plane. It does not optimize the chassis plane, upright pickup locations, shock top mount, or overall mass. Real-world selection must also check 3D hardpoints, roll center, chassis attachments, collision clearance, tire compliance, strength/buckling, damper characteristics, spring preload, and mounting loads.
